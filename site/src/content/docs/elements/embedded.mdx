These tags are accepted so that layouts with them work, and each takes up
space like its browser counterpart, but none of them does its job yet.

<Example name="el_embedded" alt="A dark video placeholder with a play button, a time and a progress line, and an empty canvas with a dashed border" />

<ElementTable tags={['canvas', 'svg', 'video', 'audio', 'iframe', 'object', 'embed']} links={false} />

| Tag | What it does today |
| --- | --- |
| `canvas` | A transparent box. There is no drawing API. |
| `svg` | A container for `foreignObject` children that host normal elements (`Element::create_svg_foreign_object`). SVG shapes such as `path` and `rect` are not tags. To show SVG artwork, register it as an `image/svg+xml` image on an [`<img>`](/elements/img/), within that format's limits. |
| `video` | With a `controls` attribute, a dark panel with video controls drawn on it; without, nothing. Nothing plays. |
| `audio` | Draws nothing; nothing plays. |
| `iframe`, `object`, `embed` | Empty boxes. Nothing can be loaded into them. |

<Aside type="note">
All of them are `inline-block` and take box styles, and `canvas`, `video`,
`iframe` and `embed` clip their content to their box. They have the
accessibility roles listed above.
</Aside>
